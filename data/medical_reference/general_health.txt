Topic: Fever
Fever is a rise in body temperature and can occur for many reasons.
Symptoms and their significance depend on the person's age, medical
history, other symptoms, and the duration of illness. A healthcare
professional can assess persistent or concerning symptoms.

Topic: Dehydration
Dehydration occurs when the body loses more fluid than it takes in.
Possible signs include thirst, dry mouth, and reduced urination.
Severe symptoms, confusion, fainting, or inability to keep fluids down
may require urgent medical assessment.

Topic: Emergency Warning Signs
Difficulty breathing, severe chest pain, sudden weakness, new confusion,
or loss of consciousness can indicate a medical emergency. Seek
emergency medical assistance rather than relying on an AI chatbot.

Topic: Professional Medical Advice
An online information tool cannot confirm a diagnosis. Users should
consult qualified healthcare professionals for individual assessment.
The urgency of care depends on the symptoms and clinical circumstances.
